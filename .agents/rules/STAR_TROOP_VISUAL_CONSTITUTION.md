- No isometric
- No angled projection
- No 3/4 perspective

## BUILDING CONSTITUTION

### Command Post (128x96)
- Federation bunker, comms mast, flags, lights
- Reads as Base HQ instantly

### Barracks
- Military prefab, sandbags, sleeping quarters
- Low wide rectangle

### Armory
- Ammo crates, weapon racks, warning lights
- Must look dangerous

### Med Bay
- Clean, white lights, medical cross
- Only bright building at night

### Research Lab
- Antenna, radar dish, scientific equipment

### Landing Pad
- Floodlights, beacon lights, dropship markings

## BUG CONSTITUTION

### Warrior Bug
- Low, wide, 6 legs, forward claws
- Readability first

### Hopper
- Mantis/locust/dragonfly
- Must read as flying immediately

### Tanker
- Beetle tank, living siege engine

### Plasma Bug
- Massive artillery creature
- Appears mostly background

### Brain Bug
- Grotesque, intelligent, unnerving
- Never cute. Never cartoon.
