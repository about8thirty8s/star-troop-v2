// ⚡ THE GOD PROMPT
DINOSAUR RUCKUS — MASTER GENERATION PROMPT
2D side-scrolling Doom-style action game. Genre DNA: Doom (1993) movement and weapon feel + Broforce chaos + Huntdown visual clarity + Terraria world density + Dead Cells enemy aggression. But with dinosaurs instead of demons.

Core aesthetic: Dark, oppressive prehistoric world. Volcanic orange and deep jungle green as dominant palette. Glowing eyes in the darkness. Bone-littered ground. Ancient ruins swallowed by jungle. Lava rivers cutting through stone. Bioluminescent caves. Every environment feels like it wants to kill you.

Player character: Lone space marine / scientist / mercenary who crash-landed on a prehistoric planet or survived a time rift. Outgunned. Outnumbered. Still winning. Movement: fast Doom-style strafe, no jump arcs — horizontal momentum king. Weapons feel HEAVY. Muzzle flashes light up the scene.

Enemy design: Dinosaurs as Doom monsters. Compys = imps (fast, weak, swarm). Raptors = shotgun zombies (medium threat, pack hunters). Dilophosaurus = acid cacodemon (projectile spitter). T-Rex = Baron of Hell equivalent (massive, charging, screen-filling). Pterodactyls = lost souls (airborne, dive-bomb). Ankylosaurus = tank (slow, massive armor, tail-sweep AOE). Every enemy has a Doom-equivalent role but feels prehistoric.

Environments: 5 biomes — Jungle Hell (green doom, dense canopy, ambushes), Volcanic Badlands (lava, fire, explosions), Bioluminescent Caves (darkness, glowing crystals, tunnel terror), Ancient Ruins (multi-level platforming, traps), Prehistoric Swamp (murky water, fog, ambush predators).

Weapons: Pistol → Shotgun → Chaingun → Rocket Launcher → BFG-equivalent "Extinction Event" cannon. Each weapon has a prehistoric flavour: rocket launcher fires fossilised bone shells, BFG fires a plasma sphere that grows to screen-filling size.

Feel targets: Movement speed = Doom (fast, momentum-based, no stopping). Enemy aggression = overwhelming hordes. Boss scale = screen-filling terror. Destruction = everything explodes satisfyingly. Pickups = dinosaur teeth (ammo), glowing amber (health), ancient tech (power-ups).

Visual identity: NOT cute. NOT cartoon. Gritty. Atmospheric. Dark. Dinosaurs rendered with weight and threat. Lighting driven by muzzle flashes, lava glow, bioluminescence, and explosions. Think Doom eternal's art direction but prehistoric. The world is hostile. The dinosaurs are terrifying. The player is the only thing more terrifying.

The tagline: "You are not the prey."

// DESIGN PILLARS
🦖 FEEL FIRST
Movement: Doom-speed horizontal momentum. No floatiness. Instant direction change.
Weapons: Heavy, impactful, screen-shaking. Each shot communicates power.
Enemies: Weight and mass. Raptors are fast and scary. T-Rex is terror.
Destruction: Explosions light up the scene. Lava splashes. Bones fly.
🌋 WORLD DESIGN
Lighting: Driven by muzzle flashes, lava, bioluminescence. Dynamic and dramatic.
Atmosphere: NOT cute. Dark, threatening, oppressive. Doom energy.
Density: Terraria-level world detail. Bones, plants, ruins, fossils everywhere.
Layers: Background / midground / foreground parallax. Deep world.
⚡ SYSTEMS
Enemy AI: Each dinosaur has a Doom-equivalent role. Predictable but threatening.
Progression: Weapon upgrades, health upgrades, speed upgrades from loot.
Boss phases: T-Rex has 3 phases, gets faster and more aggressive.
Pickups: Dino teeth = ammo. Amber = health. Ancient tech = power-ups.
