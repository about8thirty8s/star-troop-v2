Overview
Executive Summary
What this game is and why it exists
Helicopter Insertion — Every session begins here CONCEPT ART · PHASE 2 TARGET

Last Hunt: Killbox is Driftgate Studios' flagship side-scroller — a cinematic, systemic, destructible-world survival game where a small squad of elite soldiers must survive being hunted by an apex extraterrestrial predator across a procedurally generated jungle environment.

The game fuses the tension of asymmetric predator-prey gameplay, the emergent creativity of sandbox destruction, the addictive loop of roguelite progression, and the visceral feel of classic side-scrolling action. It is not a clone of anything that exists. It is its own thing.

"Predator meets Broforce meets Dead Cells meets Terraria meets Noita — and becomes something new entirely."

Design Philosophy
Core Experience Pillars
Five non-negotiables that define what this game is
01
Feel First

Every mechanic must feel elite before it ships. Movement, shooting, destruction, traversal — all must pass the "does this feel incredible?" test before anything else matters.

02
Emergent Sandbox

The world is a toolkit. Trees fall. Terrain craters. Fire spreads. Mud suppresses heat signatures. The player creates their own moments — not scripted ones.

03
Asymmetric Tension

One hunter. Multiple survivors. The power gap is real. The comeback is always possible. The tension never fully releases. This is not fair — it is balanced.

04
Roguelite Replayability

No two runs identical. Procedural world generation. Randomized loadouts. Unlockable characters. Meta-progression. The loop pulls players back relentlessly.

05
Cinematic Identity

Every session begins with a helicopter insertion. Every death is readable. Every moment of emergence should feel like it belongs in a film.

Trap Engineering PREP PHASE CORE
Prep Phase — 5 Minutes

Before the Hunter activates, the squad has five minutes. Every second matters. Harvest wood and stone. Dig tunnels. Build traps. Establish firing positions. Choose loadout. The world you build in prep becomes your battlefield.

The Hunt — It Has Begun

The Hunter activates. The sky darkens. The clock resets. The jungle stops being a construction site and becomes a killzone. Every trap you placed, every tunnel you dug, every fallen tree — this is when it matters.

The Jungle is the Equalizer

The Hunter is faster, stronger, and harder to see. It cannot, however, predict emergent physics. A well-placed falling log, a chain-trigger trap, a mud-covered ambush — the jungle is the only weapon that matches the Hunter. Not the guns.

Destruction as Gameplay — Not Just Visual

Every tree that falls changes the battlefield. A fallen tree is: a new platform, a new barrier, a new trap trigger, a new resource. A terrain explosion creates: a crater (defensive position), a fire spread (no-go zone), a debris field (cover), a permanent scar. The world is never the same after 10 minutes of play. This is the soul of the game.

Stealth Systems
Mud Camouflage
The most original mechanic in the game. Don't touch it.
Mud Camouflage — Heat signature suppressed, Hunter passes within metres STEALTH SYSTEM · ACTIVE
Why This Mechanic Is Genius

Mud camouflage suppresses the player's heat signature. The Hunter's thermal vision becomes useless. The player must remain motionless. The Hunter walks past, inches away. This is not a mechanical trick — it's a pure terror moment. Every streamer who plays this will have a clip from this system. It is genuinely original.
