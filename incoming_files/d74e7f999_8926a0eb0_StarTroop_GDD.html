01 — Elevator Pitch

Last Man Standing

You are the last ranking officer on a bug-ravaged planet. Your dropship is down. Your unit is scattered across the wasteland. By day you push outward — scouting, mining, rescuing survivors, building your perimeter. By night the bugs come. They always come. Hold the line or die screaming.

StarTroop is a Kingdom: New Lands-style 2D side-scroller set in the Starship Troopers universe. Simple to pick up, brutal to master. Every night escalates. Every survivor matters. The goal: hold out long enough to call Federation evac and get off this rock alive.

03 — World Structure

Planet P — Bug Territory

ZoneDirectionContents
Base CampCentreCommand Post, all buildable structures, your starting point
Left WingWestMining zones, underground caves, rock ore nodes, bug burrow clusters
Right WingEastRuins, crash sites, rescue pods, downed dropships, survivor camps
Deep TerritoryBoth flanksDense bug presence, rare resources, Brain Bug lair (end-game)

The world uses a single large horizontal level with the base locked to centre-screen. The camera scrolls as the player explores outward. Bug attacks come from both flanks simultaneously at night — there is no safe direction.

Sky: Perpetual red-orange alien atmosphere. Twin moons rise at dusk. At night the sky shifts to deep purple-black, lit only by your searchlights and the muzzle flash of your Morita rifles.

The Player — The Officer: Morita assault rifle, pistol sidearm, grenades. Command abilities include Rally (boosts nearby MI morale), Airstrike Call-in (Tier 3), and Retreat Signal (pulls all units behind the wall line).

06 — Enemy Codex

Know Your Bug

Five bug types. Each demands a different response. All of them want you dead.

Warrior Bug
Swarm · Melee
Fast, aggressive. Comes in overwhelming numbers. The bread and butter of every night wave. No armour — dies to sustained Morita fire.
Tanker Bug
Heavy · Fire
Slow, heavily armoured. Sprays burning fluid from its abdomen. Rare but devastating — capable of destroying structures. Prioritise with grenades and concentrated fire.
Hopper Bug
Aerial · Flanker
Winged. Bypasses all ground defenses and walls. Targets Scientists and Miners first — your non-combat units are its prey. Introduced Night 5.
Brain Bug
Boss · Psychic
Grotesque, pulsing, terrifying. Slow but unstoppable. Psychic attack disorients your units. Appears Night 15. Kill it fast — every second it lives costs you a soldier.
Plasma Bug
Artillery · Off-screen
Immobile artillery unit. Sits beyond sight range and launches plasma bolts that arc onto your base. Destroys structures in 2-3 hits. Must be hunted down proactively during the day.

10 — Win / Lose Conditions

Victory or Oblivion

☆ Victory Condition

Build the Evac Beacon at your Command Post. Defend your base for 3 consecutive nights while the beacon transmits. On the third dawn, a Federation dropship arrives. Get everyone on board. Get off this rock.

☠ Defeat Condition

The Command Post is destroyed. All units are killed. You are overrun, flanked, or simply out of ammo in the wrong moment. The bugs won this one. Restart. Learn. Come back harder.

Night AmbienceDistant bug screams. Low rumble building into wave. Searchlight hum. Heartbeat bass pulse.
MusicBasil Poledouris-inspired military orchestral. Drums-heavy during combat. Sparse, tense strings at dawn. Triumphant brass on wave clear.
UIFederation military tone stings. Clipped, crisp, efficient. No fanfare — this is a war.
